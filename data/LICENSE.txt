Changes from the source: limited to products tagged for the Netherlands that
have a Nutri-Score, with names and fields shortened, unreadable names removed,
and a quality flag added. The script that builds the file is in
https://github.com/The-Dev-Spot/basketvitality-website (scripts/build_index.py).

Flag values: 0 nothing stands out; 1 no nutrition values in this database;
2 nutrition values look inconsistent.
