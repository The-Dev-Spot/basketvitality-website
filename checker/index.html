Free shopping-list checker

Check your list before you shop.

Type one product per line to see its Nutri-Score. No account, and your list stays in your browser.

Your list isn't stored or sent anywhere. Search online appears when needed, and only sends the item you tap.

    How it works

    The page downloads a list of 18,966 products with a Nutri-Score, then searches it in your browser. Search by product name, in Dutch, and add a brand to narrow it down.

    Why some items show a range

    "Chicken fillet" isn't one product. When matching products score differently, the checker shows the range and lets you choose, instead of inventing one score.

    What it can't do

    It only knows products with a Nutri-Score in our data, and that data comes from volunteers, so some records are wrong or out of date. We warn you when a record's numbers look inconsistent. The pack is always the final word. Read how the scores work.

    A list can't tell you what the shop cost. Your receipt can.

    Scan a grocery receipt to see what it cost against your budget and what it scored. Scanning is free, with no account.
