How does muesli score?

Muesli runs the full scale from A to E. Of 146 products, 60 get a C and 53 get an A.

Last reviewed 5 November 2026

The name helps a little. Among products with crunchy or krokant in the name, 22% get an A, against 51% of the others. That's a pattern, not a rule: some crunchy mueslis still get an A. With a spread this wide, it's worth comparing the actual box. The typical product has 12 g of sugar per 100 g.

How we counted

These are the products in the database shipped with the Basket Vitality app (July 2026) with this food in their name and a Nutri-Score. We read every product name and removed bars, biscuits, yoghurt and bread rolls. Every product counts once, whatever it sells.
