How does hagelslag score?

72 of the 74 hagelslag products with a Nutri-Score get an E. The rest get a D.

The typical product here has 65 g of sugar per 100 g, which leaves little room for a better grade. Even a product labelled 30% less sugar scores E. So the score won't help you choose between brands, because nearly all of them land in the same place. It compares products, and it doesn't tell you to skip the hagelslag.

How we counted

These are the products in the database shipped with the Basket Vitality app (July 2026) with this food in their name and a Nutri-Score. We read every product name and removed nothing. Every product counts once, whatever it sells. The grades come from Open Food Facts records, which volunteers add, and we haven't checked them against each pack. Read the full method.
