How does peanut butter score?

Peanut butter (pindakaas) has a real spread: 72 products get an A and 55 get a C, out of 162.

This is a food where comparing helps. Of the 44 products with 100 in the name, usually meaning 100% peanuts, 41 get an A. An A doesn't mean low in energy, though: the typical product has 639 kcal per 100 g. The score compares peanut butter with similar foods. How we counted

Every product counts once, whatever it sells. The grades come from Open Food Facts records, which volunteers add, and we haven't checked them against each pack. Read the full method.
