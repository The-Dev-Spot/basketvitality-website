For dietitians and coaches

Start from what your client actually bought.

Basket Vitality turns a grocery receipt into a budget and a Nutri-Score overview on your client's own phone. That gives you something real to talk about.

How it can fit your work

  • Dietitians: your client scans a week's receipts, and you look at the shop together in the session.
  • Budget coaches: grocery spending appears line by line, not as one bank entry called "Supermarket".
  • Swaps, not lectures: the app suggests a few changes on the same shelf. It has no streaks, no shame and no daily reminders.

What you should know first

  • Privacy: there's no account or dashboard, and nothing reaches you or us. Your client decides what to show you on their own phone.
  • Limits: it runs on Android, in the Netherlands and Belgium. Product data is incomplete, and some lines won't get a score. See how the scores work.
  • Use your judgement: a score on every shop may not suit every client, including anyone with a history of disordered eating.

Not a medical app

Basket Vitality provides product information and general suggestions. It is not a medical app and does not give advice for medical or dietary conditions such as diabetes, allergies, or eating disorders — always consult a qualified professional.

Talk to us
