Receipt scanning

One photo of your receipt. The whole shop, explained.

Basket Vitality reads your grocery receipt on your phone and shows what it cost against your budget and what it scored on Nutri-Score.

Get Basket Vitality free

Android · On Google Play in the Netherlands and Belgium.

How it works

  1. Take a photo. No account and no typing.
  2. Your phone reads it. Text recognition happens on the device, and the receipt stays there.
  3. Each line is matched against a built-in database of more than 70,000 grocery products. A wrong match takes one tap to fix.
  4. You see the result: your budget, how much of the shop scored Nutri-D or worse, and the few swaps worth making.

How is this different from a cashback app?

Cashback apps pay you back for what you already bought. Basket Vitality doesn't pay you. It helps you see what to change next time. They do different jobs, so you can use both.

Common questions

Is it free?

Receipt and barcode scanning are free and unlimited. Free also includes the top two swaps for each shop, one budget category, the last seven days of history and one bank-statement import. Pro adds every budget category, your full history, every swap and unlimited statement imports, with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers.

Which receipts does it read?

It's built for Dutch supermarket receipts and sold on Google Play in the Netherlands and Belgium.

What if it doesn't recognise a line?

It says so instead of guessing, and you can fix the match yourself. Product data comes from Open Food Facts and RIVM's NEVO table, and neither is complete.

Does my receipt get uploaded?

No. Your receipts stay in an encrypted database on your phone. Read exactly what does go out.
